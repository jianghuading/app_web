FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about PhoenixAI.

Do I need an account?

No. PhoenixAI has no accounts and no passwords — open it and create. Chats, presets and creations all stay on your device, and uninstalling removes everything.

Which models and providers are supported?

Chat: OpenAI, DeepSeek, Doubao and more, with OpenAI / Anthropic / Gemini compatible protocols;

Images: Nano Banana, GPT Image 2, Seedream 5.0, Grok Imagine; Video: Seedance 2.0. Custom compatible endpoints are supported too, and your model list is entirely yours to curate.

What's the maximum image resolution?

Choose square (1024×1024), portrait, landscape or HD (2048×2048) canvases, 1–4 images per run — each lands in your library as it finishes.

Can videos have audio?

Yes. The Video Studio offers a “Generate Audio” toggle, 16:9 / 9:16 / 1:1 aspect ratios, 4–12 second clips at 480p–1080p, live progress — and tasks appear in your library right away.

Where do my creations live?

Everything is stored in the on-device library with full-screen preview, save-to-Photos and system sharing. Uninstalling removes it all; the app already reserves a persistence layer for future iCloud sync.

Are my API keys safe?

Yes. Keys are encrypted in the iOS Keychain and stay on your device; generation and chat requests go straight from your device to the providers you configured — we run no middle servers.
